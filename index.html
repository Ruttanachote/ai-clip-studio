AI Clip Studio

A Windows desktop app for creators of short explainer videos.

What it does

  • Helps a creator write, voice and render short vertical (9:16) explainer videos for a series.
  • Publishes a finished video to the creator’s own TikTok account. The creator picks who can view it, sets comments, duet and stitch, discloses branded or AI-generated content, previews the video and presses Post.
  • Shows each posted video’s views, likes, comments and shares next to the episode, so the creator can see what works.
  • Also publishes to the creator’s own YouTube channel and Facebook Page.

How TikTok is connected

In Settings the creator clicks “Connect TikTok” and approves access on TikTok’s own authorization page. The app uses Login Kit (user.info.basic) and the Content Posting API (video.publish), and reads statistics of the creator’s own videos (video.list). Tokens and data stay on the creator’s computer. The creator can disconnect at any time.
