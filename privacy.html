AI Clip Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

AI Clip Studio ("the app") is a desktop application for Windows that a creator installs on their own computer to make short vertical videos and publish them to their own social media accounts. The app has no server of its own and no user accounts of its own. This policy explains what the app does with data from TikTok and other platforms.

1. Data we access from TikTok

Only after you click “Connect TikTok” and approve access on TikTok’s own authorization page, the app uses these permissions:

  • user.info.basic — your display name, avatar and open ID, shown in the app so you can see which account will receive your posts.
  • video.publish — to upload a video you choose and post it to your TikTok account with the caption, privacy level and settings you pick in the app. The app never posts without you pressing the Post button.
  • video.list — to read the view, like, comment and share counts of your own videos that you linked to an episode in the app, so you can see how each episode performs.

2. Where the data is stored

Everything stays on your computer. TikTok access and refresh tokens, your basic profile and the video statistics are saved in the app’s data folder on your device. We (the developer) do not receive, collect, sell or share any of it, because the app does not send data to any server operated by us.

3. Where the data is sent

  • TikTok — the app talks directly to TikTok’s official API to sign you in, post your videos and read your video statistics.
  • Optional analysis — if you press “Analyse” (วิเคราะห์) on a series’ results page, the view/like/comment/share counts of your episodes (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook) are sent to the AI service you configured on your computer (Anthropic Claude) to write suggestions for your next episodes. This happens only when you press that button.

No TikTok data is used for advertising, sold, or shared with any other party.

4. Keeping and deleting your data

Click “Disconnect” (ยกเลิกการเชื่อม) in the app’s Settings to revoke the app’s access on TikTok and delete the stored tokens from your computer. Deleting the app’s data folder removes all stored statistics. You can also remove the app’s access at any time from your TikTok account settings (Security → Manage app permissions).

5. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required by TikTok in your country).

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
